## Known limitations (not bugs)

These came up in the security review but are explicitly out of scope for v1.x:

- **JWT staleness on role demote/delete**. A demoted user keeps their ADMIN session until JWT expiry (~30 days). Acknowledged in `app/account/actions.ts:100-104`. Proper fix is a `passwordVersion`-style mechanism — deferred.
- **Unverified-account squatting**. Signing up with someone else's email lets you log in even without verifying. Recovery is password reset (re-binds the account to whoever owns the inbox). Standard pattern.
- **Signup timing-based email enumeration**. Email-collision path skips argon2 (~100ms saving). Standard, low impact.
